Privacy Policy

Ashen Dynasty: Dragons & Heirs (Android, package com.ashendynasty.game)
Effective 16 November 2026

Ashen Dynasty: Dragons & Heirs ("the game") is made by Fenraven Studio, founded by Imrul Kayes Emon ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. The game has no accounts of its own and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information kept on your phone

Your realm, progress, settings and a short diary used to explain crashes are stored only on your phone. We do not receive them. The crash diary leaves your phone only if you choose to copy or send it to us yourself, for example when reporting a bug.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded videos and occasional ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects and shares, by itself:

  • your phone's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads and the game, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance information.

This data is sent encrypted. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Buying the Founder's Pack removes ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

Gems, the Royal Pass and other items are bought through Google Play billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so the game can give you the item.

Google Play Games

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your game progress can be saved to your Google account so you can restore it on another phone. This uses your Play Games profile and is governed by Google's privacy policy. Signing in is optional.

Notifications

The game can remind you on your phone when, for example, a building is finished. These reminders are created on your phone; no data is sent to us for them. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your phone. If you used Google Play Games saves, you can delete them from your Google account's Play Games settings. Data collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and your phone's ad settings. We hold no other data about you.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the Google services named above, the game does not share your information with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: write to us at the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play page.

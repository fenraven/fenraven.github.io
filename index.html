Meet our first game

Small studio, long games

Fenraven makes strategy games for people who like to come back to the same world for months. A kingdom you start today should still be yours, and still be interesting, three rulers later.

We are working on one game at a time. Right now that game is Ashen Dynasty: Dragons & Heirs.

  • Your save stays with youThe whole kingdom runs on your phone, so you can rule on a train, on a plane or with no signal at all.
  • Free to play, fair to playEvery dragon, rank and realm can be earned by playing. Paying only makes things faster.
  • Built from player feedbackEach release starts with a list of what players told us after the last one.

Ashen Dynasty

Dragons & Heirs

Your father has died and left you an estate, a court and one unhatched dragon egg. Build your kingdom, raise dragons and rule through the generations of your house.

Coming soonAndroid, on Google Play
Raise dragons from the egg
Hatch them, name them and grow them to their final form. Each kind fights in its own way, and a grown dragon will carry you into the sky.
Found a house that outlives you
Marry, raise children and name your heir. When the ruler dies the heir is crowned, with their own traits, and the story carries on.
Take the realm seat by seat
Six realms and eighty seats of power lie on the map. Raid, besiege, take vassals and rise through seven ranks to Emperor.
Keep a court
Lady Eva advises you, lords plot, matchmakers call, and you can grant conquered fiefs to your own children and watch them earn their titles.
Something new each week
Thirty-day seasons and weekend events such as the Dragon Moon and the Grand Tourney bring fresh rewards.
